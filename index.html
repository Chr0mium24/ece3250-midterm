考点地图Topic map

① 状态空间建模① State-space modeling L2–L4★

$\dot x = Ax+bu,\ y=cx+du$

状态选储能变量：电容电压、电感电流、位置和速度。高阶 ODE 取 $x_1=x,\ x_2=\dot x,\dots$。电路用 KCL + KVL，机械用牛顿第二定律。

Choose energy-storage variables as states: capacitor voltages, inductor currents, positions and velocities. For an $n$-th order ODE take $x_1=x,\ x_2=\dot x,\dots$. Circuits: KCL + KVL; mechanics: Newton's second law.

L3L4题型：建模Type: modeling

② 线性化② Linearization L4–L5★

$0=f(x_0,u_0),\ y_0=g(x_0,u_0);\quad A=\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\big|_0,\ b=\frac{\partial f}{\partial u}\big|_0,\ c=\frac{\partial g}{\partial x}\big|_0,\ d=\frac{\partial g}{\partial u}\big|_0$

先求工作点，再求 Jacobian，最后写成偏差变量 $\tilde x=x-x_0$ 的形式。

Find the operating point first, then the Jacobians, and write the model in deviation variables $\tilde x=x-x_0$.

③ 拉氏变换 / 部分分式③ Laplace / partial fractions L5–L6

$c_i=\lim_{s\to p_i}(s-p_i)G(s);\quad c_k=\frac{1}{(r-k)!}\lim_{s\to p}\frac{d^{r-k}}{ds^{r-k}}\big[(s-p)^rG\big]$

第二个公式用于 $r$ 重极点、$\frac{c_k}{(s-p)^k}$ 项。共轭极点：$c_3=\bar c_2$。

The second formula is for an $r$-fold pole (coefficient of $\frac{1}{(s-p)^k}$). Conjugate poles: $c_3=\bar c_2$.

④ 传递函数④ Transfer functions L7★

$G(s)=c(sI-A)^{-1}b+d,\quad \text{DC gain}=G(0)$

proper：$\deg b\le\deg a$；strictly proper：$\lt $；bi-proper：$=$。终值定理 $y(\infty)=\lim_{s\to0}sY(s)$ 要求 $sY(s)$ 的极点全在开左半平面。

Proper: $\deg b\le\deg a$; strictly proper: $\lt $; bi-proper: $=$. FVT $y(\infty)=\lim_{s\to0}sY(s)$ needs all poles of $sY(s)$ in the open left half-plane.

⑤ 方框图化简⑤ Block-diagram reduction L7–L8★

$G_2G_1,\quad G_1+G_2,\quad \dfrac{G}{1\mp GH}$

依次是串联、并联、反馈（负反馈分母为 $1+GH$）。移动求和点或引出点时补一个 $G$ 或 $1/G$。复杂图：先化简内环，再把求和点移到一起。

Series, parallel, feedback (negative feedback → denominator $1+GH$). Moving a summing or pickoff point inserts a $G$ or $1/G$. For big diagrams: close inner loops first, then merge summing junctions.

L8题型：方框图Type: block diagramHW1 2.10

⑥ 实现⑥ Realization L8–L9

控制器型：$A$ 第一行 $\big[-\tfrac{a_1}{a_0}\cdots-\tfrac{a_n}{a_0}\big]$，下面是单位次对角，$b=[\tfrac1{a_0},0,\dots,0]^T$，$c_k=b_k-b_0\tfrac{a_k}{a_0}$，$d=\tfrac{b_0}{a_0}$。观测器型是它的“转置”。

Controller form: first row of $A$ is $\big[-\tfrac{a_1}{a_0}\cdots-\tfrac{a_n}{a_0}\big]$, ones on the subdiagonal, $b=[\tfrac1{a_0},0,\dots,0]^T$, $c_k=b_k-b_0\tfrac{a_k}{a_0}$, $d=\tfrac{b_0}{a_0}$. Observer form is its “transpose”.

⑦ BIBO 稳定⑦ BIBO stability L9–L10

$\int_0^\infty|g(t)|\,dt\lt \infty\iff G\text{ proper},\ \operatorname{Re}p_i\lt 0$

证不稳定只要举一个反例（常用阶跃 $\sigma(t)$）；证稳定要对所有有界输入成立。

To show instability give one counterexample (usually the step $\sigma(t)$); stability must hold for every bounded input.

L9L10题型：BIBOType: BIBO

⑧ Routh 判据⑧ Routh criterion L10–L11★

$r_{ij}=-\dfrac{1}{r_{(i-1)0}}\det\begin{bmatrix}r_{(i-2)0}&r_{(i-2)(j+1)}\\ r_{(i-1)0}&r_{(i-1)(j+1)}\end{bmatrix}$

稳定 ⟺ 第一列全 $>0$ ⟺ 全表 $>0$。一出现 0 或负数就判不稳定。

Stable ⟺ first column all $>0$ ⟺ whole table $>0$. Any zero or negative entry ⟹ not stable.

L10题型：求 K 范围Type: K range计算器Calculator

⑨ Kharitonov⑨ Kharitonov L11★

区间多项式全部成员稳定 ⟺ 4 个顶点多项式稳定。从最高次 $a_0$ 开始，上下界排列 LLUU / LUUL / ULLU / UULL（L = 下界，U = 上界，4 位循环）。

Every member of an interval polynomial is stable ⟺ four vertex polynomials are stable. Starting from $a_0$, bounds follow LLUU / LUUL / ULLU / UULL (L = lower, U = upper, period 4).

L11题型：鲁棒稳定Type: robust stability计算器Calculator

⑩ 内稳定⑩ Internal stability L11–L12★

$P=\tfrac{b}{a},\ C=\tfrac{q}{p},\ 1+P(\infty)C(\infty)\ne0:\quad \text{internally stable}\iff ap+bq \text{ stable}$

等价于：$PC$ 中没有不稳定零极点对消，且 gang of four 中任意一个稳定。

Equivalently: no unstable pole/zero cancellation in $PC$ and any one of the gang of four is stable.

L11题型：内稳定Type: internal stability计算器Calculator

⑪ 极点配置⑪ Pole placement L12–L13★

$a(s)p(s)+b(s)q(s)=c(s),\qquad S(a,b,m+1)\begin{bmatrix}p\\q\end{bmatrix}=c$

$m$ 阶 proper 控制器能任意配置 ⟺ $m\ge n-1$；strictly proper ⟺ $m\ge n$。

An $m$-th order proper controller can assign all closed-loop poles ⟺ $m\ge n-1$; strictly proper ⟺ $m\ge n$.

易错点 Top 15Top 15 traps

  1. 方框图求和点没标 “−” 就是 “+”。课件“复杂反馈系统”的 Loop 1（$G_1\to H_1\to G_1$）是正反馈，化成 $\frac{G_1}{1-G_1H_1}$；Loop 2 标了 “−”，化成 $\frac{G_3}{1+G_3H_2}$。An unmarked summing input is “+”. In the slide's “complicated feedback system”, Loop 1 ($G_1\to H_1\to G_1$) is positive feedback: $\frac{G_1}{1-G_1H_1}$; Loop 2 has a “−”: $\frac{G_3}{1+G_3H_2}$.
  2. Routh 表出现 0 就判“不稳定”（课程不用 ε 法、也不用辅助多项式）。例：$s^4+3s^3+3s^2+3s+2=(s^2+1)(s+1)(s+2)$，$r_{30}=0$，有虚轴根。HW2 3.4(b) 同理。A zero in the Routh table means “not stable” (no ε-trick or auxiliary polynomial in this course). E.g. $s^4+3s^3+3s^2+3s+2=(s^2+1)(s+1)(s+2)$ gives $r_{30}=0$: imaginary-axis roots. Same for HW2 3.4(b).
  3. 系数全正只是必要条件。$s^3+s^2+4s+30=(s+3)(s^2-2s+10)$ 不稳定。反过来（$a_0>0$ 时）缺项或有非正系数一定不稳定。All-positive coefficients are only necessary. $s^3+s^2+4s+30=(s+3)(s^2-2s+10)$ is unstable. Conversely (with $a_0>0$) a missing or non-positive coefficient guarantees instability.
  4. 虚轴上的极点不算稳定。积分器 $\frac1s$ 输入阶跃，输出斜坡 $t\sigma(t)$ 无界。Poles on the imaginary axis are not stable. The integrator $\frac1s$ maps a step to the unbounded ramp $t\sigma(t)$.
  5. non-proper 一定不稳定。BIBO 定理要求 $G$ proper（L11 的 “Nonproper!!” 例：闭环出现 $-s-2$）。Non-proper means unstable. The BIBO theorem requires $G$ proper (L11 “Nonproper!!” example: closed loop $-s-2$).
  6. 终值定理有前提：$sY(s)$ 的极点全在开左半平面。$\frac1{s-1}$、$\frac{1}{s^2+\omega^2}$ 都不能用。FVT has a precondition: all poles of $sY(s)$ in the OLHP. It fails for $\frac1{s-1}$ and $\frac{1}{s^2+\omega^2}$.
  7. 内稳定不能只看 $\frac{PC}{1+PC}$。$P=\frac1{s-1},\ C=\frac{s-1}{s+1}$：四个里三个稳定，但 $\frac{P}{1+PC}=\frac{s+1}{(s-1)(s+2)}$ 不稳定（不稳定零极点对消）。Don't check only $\frac{PC}{1+PC}$. $P=\frac1{s-1},\ C=\frac{s-1}{s+1}$: three of four are stable but $\frac{P}{1+PC}=\frac{s+1}{(s-1)(s+2)}$ is not (unstable pole/zero cancellation).
  8. $ap+bq$ 降阶 = 不适定。$P=\frac{s+2}{s+1},\ C=-1$：$ap+bq=-1$，但 $1+P(\infty)C(\infty)=0$，闭环 non-proper，不稳定。Degree drop in $ap+bq$ = ill-posed. $P=\frac{s+2}{s+1},\ C=-1$: $ap+bq=-1$ but $1+P(\infty)C(\infty)=0$, closed loop non-proper, unstable.
  9. 期望特征多项式必须是 $n+m$ 阶，极点个数要配齐。strictly proper 多了约束 $q_0=0$，最低要 $m=n$。The desired polynomial must have degree $n+m$ (count the poles). Strictly proper adds $q_0=0$, so the minimum is $m=n$.
  10. 控制器本身可以不稳定。$C=\frac{33s-6}{s-25}$ 不稳定，但闭环极点 $-1,-2,-3$ 稳定。问 “Stable controller?” 时单独看 $p(s)$ 的根。The controller itself may be unstable. $C=\frac{33s-6}{s-25}$ is unstable while the closed loop ($-1,-2,-3$) is stable. For “Stable controller?” check the roots of $p(s)$.
  11. 线性化顺序：先解工作点（包括 $u_0$），再求偏导，最后代入；结果写成 $\tilde x,\tilde u,\tilde y$。MagLev 的 $u_0=Ry_0\sqrt{2Mg/(rL_1)}$ 别漏。Linearization order: solve the operating point (including $u_0$), differentiate, then substitute; write the result in $\tilde x,\tilde u,\tilde y$. Don't forget $u_0=Ry_0\sqrt{2Mg/(rL_1)}$ for MagLev.
  12. $\L[\dot x]=sX$ 用的是 $0^-$ 下限。$(\cos\omega t)\sigma(t)$ 求导会出现 $\delta(t)$。$\L[\dot x]=sX$ uses the $0^-$ lower limit. Differentiating $(\cos\omega t)\sigma(t)$ produces a $\delta(t)$.
  13. 课程的分类口径：比例是 static，积分是 dynamic；PI 是 causal，微分器 $K_D\dot u$ 是 noncausal（L2 原话）。Course conventions: proportional = static, integral = dynamic; PI is causal, the differentiator $K_D\dot u$ is noncausal (as stated in L2).
  14. Kharitonov 模式从最高次 $a_0$ 开始数；先把闭环特征多项式写出来，再看每个系数的区间。Kharitonov patterns start at the leading coefficient $a_0$; write the closed-loop characteristic polynomial first, then read each coefficient's interval.
  15. 实现不唯一。$(TAT^{-1},Tb,cT^{-1},d)$ 给出同一个传递函数，所以 ss(tf(sys)) 和原矩阵、和手写的控制器型都可以不同（HW1 2.16(e)）。Realizations are not unique. $(TAT^{-1},Tb,cT^{-1},d)$ has the same transfer function, so ss(tf(sys)), the original matrices and your controller form can all differ (HW1 2.16(e)).
